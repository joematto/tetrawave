SHENEgyptian Shen

As the "Symbol of All Symbols" the Shen identifies the place of transition or passing from Time to Eternity. In ancient Summaria, the Goddess Ianana (Ishtar) is holding the Shen which was later adopted by the Egyptians.

The Proportions of the Shen can be seen to relate to the Core Aspect of TetraWave in 2 distinct ways:

1. The Notion of "Tangent"...a Line Tangent to a Circle.
2. The Ratio of the Length of the Line to the Diameter of the Circle.
